## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of computational engineering, molecular genomics, and cellular biology. The visual language merges clinical clarity with structural digital precision—delivering an atmosphere that is scientific, authoritative, trustworthy, and technologically sophisticated.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Clinical Rigor & Precision:** Sharp typographic hierarchy, calibrated alignments, and disciplined spacing mirror the exacting standards of biotechnology and data analytics.
- **Biocomputational Synergy:** Hexagonal motifs, circuit traces, and helical bio-structures harmonize modern life-sciences research with enterprise cloud computing.
- **Clarity & Transparency:** Dominant crisp white surfaces paired with subtle cool off-white layers create a laboratory-clean environment where critical data points, telemetry, and sequences take absolute focus.

### Design Movement
**Modern Corporate / Precision Technical:** Built upon crisp grid structures, low-contrast cool-grey borders, and subtle tinted surface shifts. It rejects hyper-decorative trends in favor of refined utility, pristine whitespace, and technical credibility suited for enterprise biopharma platforms and diagnostic suites.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects life-science instrumentation and computational intelligence. High-contrast deep navy ensures legibility and stability, while luminous cyan-teals inject energy into key metrics, active states, and data visualizations.

### Primary Color: Deep Blue (`#0B315B`)
The structural backbone. Used for high-emphasis typography, primary action buttons, main navigation shells, and dominant brand marks. It projects deep authority, institutional confidence, and security.

### Secondary Color: Cyan Gradient Accent (`#3BAAC6`)
The computational vector. Used for primary focus highlights, active tabs, progress telemetry, interactive graph nodes, and directional accents. Often paired with `#0B315B` in linear bi-color accents.

### Tertiary Color: Soft Cyan / Aqua (`#72C0D4`)
Used for interactive secondary states, auxiliary metric highlights, pill backgrounds in interactive filter states, and data series overlays.

### Neutral & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure clinical white for main analytical surfaces and content sheets.
- **Surface Muted (`#F4F7FA`):** Cool tinted off-white used for sidebar foundations, table headers, inactive card containers, and dashboard background canvas.
- **Neutral Accent Border (`#B0C4D9`):** Subdued metallic grey-blue used for structural dividers, inactive card outlines, and gridlines.
- **Neutral Body (`#5C6F81`):** Calibrated slate-grey for body prose, secondary metadata, table labels, and descriptive captions.

## Typography

The typography system uses **Hanken Grotesk** for structural display headings and **Work Sans** for balanced, highly legible body copy and technical telemetry.

### Typographic Roles & Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in **Hanken Grotesk** with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and firm weights (600/700). Evokes engineering rigor, precision instruments, and clarity.
- **Body & Data Displays:** Set in **Work Sans** with open letterforms, standard proportions, and generous vertical rhythm (`1.5` to `1.6` relative line-height). Ensures effortless readability across analytical reports, genomic assays, and complex table rows.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Work Sans in medium to semibold weights with uppercase letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for status badges, column identifiers, and technical metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a 12-column fluid grid system optimized for information-dense biosciences dashboards and scientific workstations.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12 columns with a `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutter and `2.5rem` (`40px`) outer canvas margin. Complex analytics dashboards employ nested 6- or 4-column module groups.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `1.5rem` (`24px`) margin. Analytical charts and diagnostic tables collapse into stacked pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) edge margin. Multi-column metric cards stack into single-column flows.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals are aligned to a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm:
- `space-xs` (`4px`): Micro badge gaps, tight icon-to-label intervals.
- `space-sm` (`8px`): Form field vertical interior padding, chip horizontal paddings.
- `space-md` (`16px`): Standard card body inset, table cell padding, input gap.
- `space-lg` (`24px`): Structural section spacing within cards, panel-to-panel offset.
- `space-xl` (`40px`): Macro canvas segment separation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, instead employing subtle tonal shifts, crisp low-contrast borders, and diffused cool-blue ambient shadows to represent visual layers.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Background workspace rendered in `#F4F7FA`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp `1px` solid border (`#B0C4D9` at 50% opacity). No resting shadow needed in standard state.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards & Popovers):** Ambient cool-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 49, 91, 0.08)`. Border shifts to tertiary `#72C0D4`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Drawers & Flyouts):** High-clarity elevated shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(11, 49, 91, 0.16)`. Combined with a subtle `rgba(11, 49, 91, 0.25)` frosted backdrop blur (`blur(4px)`).

### Ghost Outlines & Micro-Depth
To honor laboratory and diagnostic instrumentation aesthetics, dividers and perimeter frames are kept razor-sharp at 1px using `#B0C4D9`. Never use deep black dropshadows; all shadow penumbras must retain the `#0B315B` tinting.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`), balancing technical efficiency with modern ergonomics.

### Curvature Rules
- **Interactive Controls & Inputs:** `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius. Buttons, form controls, and select triggers retain crisp, structured contours reflecting software precision.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) (`rounded-lg`). Provides clean separation between modules without appearing overly playful or soft.
- **Modals & Overlays:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) (`rounded-xl`). Used sparingly for floating macro containers.
- **Status Pills & Micro Chips:** Pure pill radius (`9999px`) reserved strictly for status indicators (e.g., "Active Assay", "Sequenced") to contrast against orthogonal card geometries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0B315B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`, border-radius `4px`, padding `10px 20px`. Hover transitions to a linear gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #0B315B 0%, #3BAAC6 100%)`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0B315B`, border `1px solid #B0C4D9`, border-radius `4px`. Hover triggers surface change to `#F4F7FA` with border tint `#72C0D4`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#3BAAC6`, font weight `600`. Hover adds subtle `#F4F7FA` pill backing.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicator Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `24px`, padding `2px 10px`, font size `11px`, tracking `0.05em`, uppercase.
- **Active State:** Background `rgba(59, 170, 198, 0.12)`, text `#0B315B`, border `1px solid #72C0D4`.
- **Neutral State:** Background `#F4F7FA`, text `#5C6F81`, border `1px solid #B0C4D9`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #B0C4D9`, border-radius `4px`, color `#0B315B`, placeholder `#5C6F81` (60% opacity).
- **Focus State:** Border color `#3BAAC6`, subtle ambient ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 170, 198, 0.20)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radio Controls:** `18px × 18px`, border `1.5px solid #B0C4D9`, border-radius `3px` (radios `50%`). Checked state fills with `#0B315B` displaying a crisp white micro checkmark.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(176, 196, 217, 0.6)`, border-radius `8px`, padding `24px`.
- **Header:** Bottom-bordered with `1px solid #F4F7FA`, featuring display titles in `#0B315B` accompanied by secondary metric labels in `#5C6F81`.

### Data Tables & Genomic Sequences
- **Headers:** Background `#F4F7FA`, text `#0B315B`, font size `12px`, uppercase, tracking `0.05em`, height `36px`, border-bottom `1px solid #B0C4D9`.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle row hover `rgba(114, 192, 212, 0.08)`, border-bottom `1px solid #F4F7FA`, text `#5C6F81`, numerical data aligned right with tabular figures.
